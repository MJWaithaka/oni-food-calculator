Conditions & calculation notes

    Farm growth assumes suitable temperature, atmosphere and pressure. No spoilage, travel time, power, cooling or cooking labor is included. Gas Range fuel is not budgeted. Smoker fuel is included. External supplies are endpoints: this is a food planner, not a simulation of every industrial resource loop.

    Ranch estimate: surplus per adult = 1 ÷ egg interval − 1 ÷ adult lifespan. Reserve enough eggs to replace adults. Rounded counts are construction suggestions; increase the surplus to budget headroom. Wild applies to plants only. Fertilizer costs include synthesizer inputs, without crediting byproducts.
