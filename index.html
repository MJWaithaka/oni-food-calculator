How to

  1. Input normal and Bottomless Stomach dupe counts.
  2. Select the food you want to feed your colony.
  3. Choose whether plants are dupe-harvested, wild, or fertilized.

Guidance

The tool assumes no downtime between actions. Plants are harvested and fertilized immediately when those options are selected. Consider budgeting an extra dupe or two as a buffer. Wild plants take four times longer but consume no resources; unattended ripe plants wait four cycles before dropping their harvest.
